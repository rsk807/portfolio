Research & Innovation

Academic writing, journals, publications, and system modeling.

Academic research provides the mathematical and structured foundation necessary to build secure architectures. Rather than treating theory and implementations as separate stages, my work focuses on translating models directly into physical microcontrollers or client-edge algorithms.

My research catalog includes publishing in a Springer Journal for VACARS (Voice Assisted Context Aware Response System), an IoT + AI assisted smart helmet system, and a Published Indian Patent Application (Patent ID: 202521077464, Indian Patent Office (InPASS)) for Omniscient (Digital Forensics & Cybercrime Investigation Platform).
